How to permanently delete your DermaCare account and data.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open DermaCare and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom and confirm.

Deletion is immediate.

Without the app

Email us from the address you signed up with, and we will delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

Email a deletion request

buildwithhammad@gmail.com

What is deleted

Your profile and photo, skin photos, AI results, cases, family profiles, messages, appointments and notifications are permanently removed. Doctors' licence documents are removed too.

If you are a doctor, patients you treated keep their own case history, which shows your name as "account closed". Nothing is kept for advertising or analytics.
