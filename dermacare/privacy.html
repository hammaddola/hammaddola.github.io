DermaCare

Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

1. In short

DermaCare collects only what it needs to screen your skin and connect you with a dermatologist. Your photos and health details are never sold, never used for advertising, and are seen only by you, the dermatologist handling your case, and our platform administrators when needed for safety.

2. What we collect

Account details: your name, email address and password (stored encrypted by our authentication provider).

Profile details you choose to add: photo, phone number, address, date of birth and gender.

Health information: skin photos you take, symptoms you describe, AI screening results, doctor notes, chat messages and appointment history.

Family profiles: names and details of family members you add and scan on their behalf.

Doctors also provide their licence number and licence document for verification.

3. How we use it

To run an AI screening of your skin photo and show you the result and care guidance.

To let a verified dermatologist review your case, chat with you and hold video or in-person appointments.

To create your PDF report, entirely on your phone.

To keep the service safe: preventing misuse, limiting automated requests and verifying doctors.

4. Who processes your data

Supabase hosts our database, file storage and sign-in. Data is protected by row-level security, so each account can only reach the records it is allowed to see.

Google Gemini analyses skin photos and writes care guidance. Photos are sent for analysis only and are not used by DermaCare for advertising.

Stream provides video calls between you and your doctor.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

5. Who can see your case

You, and family members only through your own account.

Verified dermatologists: a case is visible to doctors while it is waiting for review, and afterwards only to the doctor who accepted it.

DermaCare administrators, only to verify doctors, handle reports and keep the service safe.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active, so your skin history and reports stay available.

When you delete your account, your profile, photos, cases, messages and appointments are permanently removed. If a doctor deletes their account, the patient keeps their case history, which then shows the doctor's name as "account closed".

7. Your rights and choices

View and edit your profile at any time in Profile > Edit Profile.

Download any case as a PDF report from the case screen.

Delete your account in the app (Profile > Delete Account) or by emailing us from your account email.

Withdraw consent for AI screening at any time by not using the scan feature; you can still consult a doctor.

8. Security

Connections are encrypted. Photos are stored in private storage and shown only through short-lived links. Sessions are kept in your phone's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

9. Children

You must be 18 or older to create an account. Parents and guardians can add children as family profiles and remain responsible for their use.

10. Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date above and tell you in the app for important changes.
