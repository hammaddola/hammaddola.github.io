1. Not a diagnosis

DermaCare's AI screening gives a possible condition and general care guidance. It is not a medical diagnosis and can be wrong. Only a qualified dermatologist can diagnose you.

Always follow your doctor's advice. Do not delay or stop treatment because of an AI result.

2. Not for emergencies

If you have a severe reaction, difficulty breathing, rapid swelling, heavy bleeding, a fast-spreading or very painful rash, or a high fever with a rash, go to the nearest emergency department or call 1122. Do not wait for a reply in the app.
