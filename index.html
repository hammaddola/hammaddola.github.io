Featured case study · Real product

DermaCare: from a one-minute skin scan to a real dermatologist

A telemedicine app where patients scan a skin concern with AI, then get a licence-verified dermatologist on it by chat, video or an in-person visit. Patients pay securely by JazzCash, Easypaisa or card when they book. Patient, doctor and admin apps from one Flutter codebase.

  • Flutter
  • Bloc
  • Supabase
  • PostgreSQL RLS
  • Gemini
  • TensorFlow
  • Deno
  • Stream Video
  • JazzCash
  • Easypaisa
01

The challenge

People put off seeing a dermatologist because they can't tell whether a spot is serious, and medical apps have to be private, safe and trusted from the first screen.

02

AI that knows its limits

The AI checks each photo first: blurry or non-skin photos get a retake tip instead of a guess. Anything that could be serious, including possible cancer signs, is flagged for a dermatologist to review, never left to the AI alone. A backup model keeps it working when traffic spikes.

03

Security by design

Each patient can only ever see their own records and photos. Doctors are licence-checked by an admin before they can take cases. Prices are set on the server and every payment is double-checked with the gateway before the doctor's share is booked, so fees can't be tampered with.

04

Crafted, tested, shipped

Smooth, accessible screens in English and Urdu, built for screen readers too. Over 200 automated tests, including a 41-step run through the live system, check every release before it reaches users.

05

The result

A complete product in final launch preparation: patient, doctor and admin apps on one live backend, JazzCash, Easypaisa and card payments built in, a signed Android release build, and public privacy, terms and account-deletion pages ready for store review.
