Featured case study · My own product

DermaCare: a healthcare app built around safe AI and real doctors

A telemedicine app where patients get an AI-assisted first check of a skin concern, then a licence-checked dermatologist by chat, video or an in-person visit, and pay by JazzCash, Easypaisa or card when they book. The AI supports triage; it never gives a diagnosis.

  • Flutter
  • Supabase
  • Gemini AI
  • Video calls
  • JazzCash
  • Easypaisa
My role
Sole developer: product design, app, backend, AI, payments and testing
What I built
Patient, doctor and admin apps on one secure backend, for Android, iOS and web
Problem solved
People delay seeing a dermatologist because they can't tell if a spot is serious
Status
Final launch preparation: signed release build, store policies published
01

The challenge

Make it easy to get a worrying spot looked at, while staying private, safe and trusted from the first screen, as a medical app must.

02

AI that knows its limits

The AI checks each photo first: blurry or non-skin photos get a retake tip instead of a guess. Anything that could be serious, including possible cancer signs, is flagged for a dermatologist to review, never left to the AI alone. A backup model keeps it working when traffic spikes.

03

Patient data stays private

Each patient can only ever see their own records and photos. Doctors are licence-checked by an admin before they can take cases. Prices are set on the server and every payment is double-checked with the gateway before the doctor's share is booked, so fees can't be tampered with.

04

Crafted, tested, shipped

Smooth, accessible screens in English and Urdu, built for screen readers too. A 41-step automated run through the live system checks every release before it reaches users.

05

The result

A complete product in final launch preparation: patient, doctor and admin apps on one live backend, JazzCash, Easypaisa and card payments built in, a signed Android release build, and public privacy, terms and account-deletion pages ready for store review.
