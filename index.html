Featured case study

DermaCare: skin health, from a one-minute scan to a real doctor

A telemedicine app where patients scan a skin concern with AI, then get a licence-verified dermatologist on it by chat, video or an in-person visit. Patient, doctor and admin apps from one Flutter codebase.

  • Flutter
  • Bloc
  • Supabase
  • PostgreSQL RLS
  • Gemini
  • TensorFlow
  • Deno
  • Stream Video
01

The challenge

People put off seeing a dermatologist because they can't tell whether a spot is serious, and medical apps have to be private, safe and trusted from the first screen.

02

AI that knows its limits

Gemini vision triage with a strict schema. It rejects blurry or non-skin photos with a retake tip, and always sends any cancer signal to a doctor. Retries and a fallback model keep it working under load.

03

Security by design

Row-level security on every table, admin-verified doctors, private photo storage, and API keys kept server-side in edge functions, never inside the app.

04

Crafted, tested, shipped

A motion system, painted 3D visuals and accessible UI. 79 automated checks, including a 33-step live end-to-end suite, plus CI builds for Android and iOS.
